---
title: About Pelota
---

**Pelota** is a free and open source 3D tennis game made with the [Godot engine](https://godotengine.org/).
Its goal is tennis that feels like tennis: a ball with real physics, players who move like
athletes, strokes that reward timing and footwork, and an opponent that thinks.

*Pelota* is Spanish for *ball*.

## The project

Pelota is developed in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/mbkma/pelota) by
[mbkma](https://github.com/mbkma) and contributors. Everyone is welcome to
[help](../docs/contribute/).
