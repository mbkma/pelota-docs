Directions always follow the screen: pushing **up** moves you up the screen, no matter which end
of the court you are on.

| What | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move / aim | <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or arrow keys | Left stick or D-pad |
| Topspin shot, flat serve | <kbd>Space</kbd> | <kbd>A</kbd> |
| Slice shot, slice serve | <kbd>Shift</kbd> | <kbd>X</kbd> |
| Drop shot (drop volley at the net), kick serve | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> | <kbd>Y</kbd> |
| Ask the ball machine for a ball (training) | <kbd>E</kbd> | Right trigger |
| Pause / menu | <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Start |

## Rally: one button press, three jobs

When the opponent's ball comes, **press and hold** a shot button. From that moment:

1. **Your player moves to the ball on their own**, but only at a jogging pace. You should already
   be close: good footwork before you press is what makes the shot good.
2. **The direction now aims** the shot instead of moving you. A ring on the opponent's side shows
   where the ball will land.
3. **Holding the button charges power.** About 0.8 seconds of holding gives the full attacking
   pace.

**Release the button just before the racket meets the ball.** That release is your timing:
the closer to contact, the better (see [Hitting well](../hitting-well/)). If you are still
holding the button when the racket meets the ball, you play a weak, safe ball short into the
middle of the court.

## Serve

1. Before the serve, left and right slide you along the baseline.
2. **Press and hold** a serve button to choose the serve: <kbd>Space</kbd> flat, <kbd>Shift</kbd>
   slice, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> kick. While you hold it, the direction aims into the service box.
3. **Release** to toss the ball.
4. **Press any serve button again right before the racket meets the ball.** The closer to the
   contact, the faster and more precise the serve. A serve you do not time is slow and wild.
