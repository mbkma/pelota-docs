The Pelota documentation has been rewritten from scratch. It is now about the **ideas** behind
the game rather than the code, and it is written for everyone: players, curious tennis fans, and
contributors.

<!--more-->

Some highlights:

- **[Hitting well]({{< relref "/docs/play/hitting-well/" >}})**: how timing, position and choosing to take the
  ball early or late make a perfect shot.
- **[Pressure and confidence]({{< relref "/docs/how-it-works/mental-game/" >}})**: why defending break points
  weighs on a player, and why being hopelessly behind can free them up.
- **[Animation]({{< relref "/docs/how-it-works/animation/" >}})**: how motion-captured running is kept in step so
  the feet never slide, and how a swing is timed to meet the ball.
- **[The AI opponent]({{< relref "/docs/how-it-works/ai/" >}})**: how the computer reads your shot, punishes short
  balls and decides when to come to the net.
- **[Contribute]({{< relref "/docs/contribute/" >}})**: guides for programmers, artists, animators, translators,
  writers and testers.

The plots in the docs are drawn by a script that repeats the game's formulas, so they stay true
when the game changes. Found something wrong? Every page has an **Edit this page** link.
